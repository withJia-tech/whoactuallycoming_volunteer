The source's threshold values have not been treated as validated targets. Minimum subgroup sizes, complement comparison, date-only approval cutoffs and one-primary-slot scope are **new proposed conventions**. They must be accepted or revised before the pilot, not after looking at results.

The implementation boundary is local calculation and synthetic verification. Human owners remain responsible for approving the protocol, resolving identities/replies, operating the existing messaging workflow and attesting observed facts. No credentials, integrations, persistent service, AI matching, identity inference, automated sends or real volunteer data are added.
