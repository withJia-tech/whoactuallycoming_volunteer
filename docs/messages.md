# Private confirmation drafts

These reproduce the v1.1 message intent. Organisers fill the existing Sheet/Zap fields manually. **Native-speaker review and any sender approvals are pending.** Verify actual connector requirements with its owner before use. No code here sends these messages. Do not send to a group.

## English

Hi [First name], you're on [role] for [event] on [day, date], [time] at [venue]. Please reply 1 to confirm, or 2 if you can't make it. If we don't hear from you by [deadline], we'll offer the slot to someone else, so thank you for letting us know!

## 简体中文

[名字] 您好！您在 [活动] 担任 [岗位]，时间：[日期] [时间]，地点：[地点]。请回复 1 确认，或回复 2 表示无法出席。如在 [截止时间] 前未收到回复，我们会把这个岗位安排给其他义工，谢谢您！

## Bahasa Melayu

Salam [Nama], anda bertugas sebagai [peranan] untuk [acara] pada [hari, tarikh], [masa] di [tempat]. Sila balas 1 untuk sahkan, atau 2 jika tidak dapat hadir. Jika tiada balasan sebelum [masa tamat], kami akan tawarkan tugas ini kepada sukarelawan lain, terima kasih!

## Interpretation

Accept any clear yes or no; do not require an exact keyword. Log text, emoji or voice form without retaining message content or audio. A person, not a model or script, resolves meaning. Preserve the deadline snapshot and log late replies separately through `ever_replied`. The Tamil requirement remains a human decision; no unreviewed Tamil translation is supplied.
